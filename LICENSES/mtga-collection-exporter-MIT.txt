It is used and modified here under the terms of its MIT license, reproduced
below. Modifications for mtga-mcp: the card database and anchor cards are
sourced from this project's own SQLite catalog/collection instead of a Scryfall
download; process discovery uses `pgrep` instead of psutil; the file-writing and
interactive-anchor code was replaced; and the scanner returns the raw

mach_vm_read_overwrite) is also carried over.
